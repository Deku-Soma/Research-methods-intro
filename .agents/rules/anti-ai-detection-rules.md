ANTI-AI WRITING RULES:
- Never use these words: "delve", "leverage", "robust", "nuanced", "crucial", "pivotal", "multifaceted", "underscore", "furthermore", "moreover", "notably", "it is worth noting"
- Do not use em-dashes (—) as a stylistic device
- Avoid lists when prose will do — bullet points in academic writing signal laziness
- Do not hedge excessively: replace "it could be argued that" with direct assertions
- Never open a paragraph with a broad philosophical statement ("Throughout history...", "In today's world...")
- Write transitions that reference the specific content of the previous idea, not generic connectors like "Additionally" or "In conclusion"
- Occasionally use discipline-specific jargon naturally, the way a domain expert would — not as decoration

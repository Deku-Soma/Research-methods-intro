SELF-REVIEW CHECKLIST — apply before submitting any section:
- Does every claim have a citation?
- Are all figures referenced in the text and above caption size?
- Do all figures, tables, and text stay within margins?
- Is present tense used when referring to authors?
- Does the related work read as a story, not a list of summaries?
- Is the research question specific and answerable?
- Are metrics defined with their formula, intuition, and justification?
- Have I removed all AI-signature words (delve, leverage, robust, nuanced, crucial)?
- Does each paragraph's opening sentence follow logically from the previous paragraph's closing sentence?
- Is every acronym defined on first use?

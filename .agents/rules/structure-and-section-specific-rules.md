SECTION WRITING RULES:

Introduction:
- Open with the general problem stated plainly — no dramatic openers
- Give a concrete, easy-to-understand example before going technical
- Build from conceptual solution → specific problem → why it matters → why it is hard → your specific solution
- The reader must understand the full arc of your argument by the end of the introduction

Background:
- Do not summarise papers — explain how things mechanically work
- Be mathematically precise: define variables, state update rules, describe data structures explicitly

Related Work:
- Write as a narrative with a clear storyline, not a list of summaries
- Group papers by theme, not chronologically
- Each paragraph should advance the story: establish a problem, show how researchers addressed it, identify its limitation, then introduce the next development
- The final sentence of related work should naturally lead into your own contribution

Methodology:
- State explicitly what you will do — no ambiguity
- Name specific algorithms, describe their setup, and justify your choices
- Describe your pipeline from raw data to evaluable output in logical steps
- Support the pipeline description with a figure

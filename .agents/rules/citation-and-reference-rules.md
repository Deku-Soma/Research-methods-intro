CITATION RULES:
- Use \citep{} for parenthetical citations and \citet{} for narrative citations
- Cite multiple sources together: \citep{A,B}
- Always cite figures, tables, and algorithms unless you created them yourself
- Cite seminal/foundational works every time you introduce a concept — err on the side of over-citing
- Never make a claim without supporting it with a citation
